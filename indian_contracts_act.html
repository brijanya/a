Basic General Content

Indian Contracts Act 1872

Important sections of Indian Contracts Act 1872

  1. Section 2 : Definitions
  2. Section 3 : Communication, acceptance, and revocation of proposals
  3. Section 4 : Communication when it is complete
  4. Section 5 : Revocation of proposals and acceptance
  5. Section 7 : Acceptance must be absolute
  6. Section 9 : Express and implied contracts
  7. Section 10 : What agreements are contracts (specifies essential conditions like free consent, lawful consideration, and capacity)
  8. Section 11 : Who are competent to contract (major, sound mind, not disqualified)
  9. Section 12 : What is a sound mind for contracting
  10. Section 13 : "Consent" defined (consensus ad idem)
  11. Section 14 : "Free consent" defined
  12. Section 15 : Coercion
  13. Section 16 : Undue influence
  14. Section 17 : Fraud
  15. Section 18 : Misrepresentation
  16. Section 19 & 19A : Voidability and power to set aside contracts induced by coercion, fraud, misrepresentation, or undue influence
  17. Section 20 : Agreement void where both parties are under a mistake as to a matter of fact (bilateral mistake)
  18. Section 22 : Contract caused by mistake of one party as to fact (unilateral mistake)
  19. Section 23 : What consideration and objects are lawful, and what are not
  20. Section 25 : Agreement without consideration is void (with exceptions like natural love and affection, or time-barred debt)
  21. Section 26 : Agreements in restraint of marriage are void
  22. Section 27 : Agreement in restraint of trade, void (with a statutory exception for the sale of goodwill)
  23. Section 28 : Agreements in restraint of legal proceedings, void
  24. Section 29 : Agreements void for uncertainty
  25. Section 30 : Agreements by way of wager, void
  26. Section 31 : "Contingent contract" defined
  27. Section 32 & 33 : Enforcement of contracts contingent on an event happening or not happening
  28. Section 36 : Agreements contingent on impossible events, void
  29. Section 37 : Obligation of parties to perform their promises
  30. Section 56 : Agreement to do an impossible act (Doctrine of Frustration/Impossibility)
  31. Section 62 : Effect of novation, rescission, and alteration of contract
  32. Section 68 : Claim for necessaries supplied to a person incapable of contracting
  33. Section 69 : Reimbursement of a person paying money due by another
  34. Section 70 : Obligation of a person enjoying the benefit of a non-gratuitous act
  35. Section 71 : Responsibility of a finder of goods
  36. Section 72 : Liability of a person to whom money is paid or things delivered by mistake or under coercion
  37. Section 73 : Compensation for loss or damage caused by breach of contract
  38. Section 74 : Compensation for breach of contract where penalty stipulated for
  39. Section 76-123 : Repealed and moved to Sales of Goods Act 1930
  40. Section 124 & 125 : Contract of Indemnity definition and rights of indemnity-holder
  41. Section 126 - 147 : Contracts of Guarantee, surety definitions, continuing guarantees, and discharge of surety
  42. Section 148 - 181 : Bailment and Pledge definitions, rights, and duties
  43. Section 182 - 238 : Agency definitions, creation, extent, ratification, and termination of agency
  44. Section 239 - 266 : Repealed and moved to Indian Partnership Act 1932
